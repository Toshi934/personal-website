HTE & Meta-Learner Series

A seven-part series on estimating heterogeneous treatment effects using meta-learner frameworks. Each video covers one algorithm — from the intuition behind the estimator to its step-by-step computation.

Part 1 of 7
Causal Inference Machine Learning

Introduction to Heterogeneous Treatment Effects

Introduces the concept of heterogeneous treatment effects and explains why individual-level causal estimates matter beyond the average treatment effect. Covers the CATE estimand and motivates the meta-learner framework.

Part 2 of 7
S-Learner

S-Learner

Estimates CATE using a single outcome model that includes treatment as a covariate, then imputes counterfactuals by toggling the treatment indicator.

Part 3 of 7
T-Learner

T-Learner

Fits separate outcome models for the treated and untreated groups, then subtracts predicted outcomes to estimate individual treatment effects.

Part 4 of 7
X-Learner

X-Learner

Extends the T-Learner with a cross-fitting step that uses imputed treatment effects from each group to improve estimation in imbalanced samples.

Part 5 of 7
DR-Learner

DR-Learner

Constructs doubly-robust pseudo-outcomes combining outcome model predictions and inverse probability weighting, then regresses these on covariates for efficient CATE estimation.

Part 6 of 7
R-Learner

R-Learner

Applies Robinson's partial linear decomposition to residualize both outcome and treatment, then estimates CATE from residual-on-residual regression with sample weights.

Part 7 of 7
BCF

Bayesian Causal Forest

Uses two separate BART ensembles — one for the prognostic function and one for the treatment effect — with strong regularization priors that shrink toward zero.
